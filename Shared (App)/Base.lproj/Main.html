Keyword Search

To enable the Safari extension, tap the page menu button at the left of the address bar and choose “Manage Extensions”.

When Safari asks, choose “Always Allow…” and allow Keyword Search on your search engine’s website, so it can see your searches. If you switch search engines now and then, choose “Always Allow on Every Website” instead.

Keyword Search only looks at the address of pages you open to recognize searches. Nothing is stored, collected or sent anywhere, and there is no analytics or tracking. Only your list of searches is synced, through your own iCloud account.

Now try typing e.g. “y Rick Astley” in the Safari address bar to try a keyword search.

To edit your searches and settings, open the page menu and choose “Keyword Search”.
